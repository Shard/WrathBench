# 2026-08-30

## The two smokes that failed on the first `:latest` run were smoke bugs, not module bugs

The `:next` module (items 95–103, build harness-0.5-268-g303b4ec-dirty) went live as `:latest` at the end of 2026-08-29, and the first sequential smoke run on PROBE failed `reputation.ts` and `item-stats.ts`. Both were wrong on the smoke side; the decodes are right and no module change is owed.

- `infra/smoke/reputation.ts` read `state.reputation()` synchronously, 0.1 s after `createSession` resolved. `SMSG_INITIALIZE_FACTIONS` is sent during login (before `createSession` returns) and the observation stream folds it asynchronously, so the read raced the fold — the same race skills.ts avoids with its post-login wait. `/health` showed no `droppedByOpcode` and the module indexes 105 factions at startup, so nothing was dropped or untapped. The smoke now polls for the first fold (10 s cap). Live on SMOKE3: 89 rows, 6 visible, Stormwind Friendly 4000 for a fresh human, Orgrimmar Hated and at war.
- `infra/smoke/item-stats.ts` looked for a chest piece; a fresh Human Paladin wears shirt (3), pants (6), boots (7) and hammer (15), no chest. It also demanded `maxDurability` on every worn piece, but `item_template` gives Recruit's/Squire's Shirt and Squire's Boots armor 0 and MaxDurability 0 — the wire's zeros were the decode being right. The smoke now asserts armor and durability on the pants, damage/speed/durability on the weapon, bonding present on every piece. Live on SMOKE3: Battleworn Hammer 3–5 @ 2900 ms, Squire's Pants armor 2 / durability 25.

Root `bun test` (2571 tests) and `bun run typecheck` green.
